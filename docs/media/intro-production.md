Produced on 2026-11-13 from actual Spektramat Android preview UI. The videos use Chinese titles and English subtitles, with a bilingual SRT sidecar. The portrait edition is composed separately; it is not a crop of the landscape edition.

## Real app footage

All screenshots and recordings were captured through ADB from `emulator-5554`, running `com.spektra.latent.dev`. No real phone was operated. Existing QA projects and photographs were retained; five test JPEG copies were imported into the debug inbox for the demonstration. The emulator was restored to its original 945 × 2100 override size and density 420 after capture.

`intro-source/` contains five compressed JPEG screen captures and three compressed app recordings, totalling 1,313,621 bytes before the source manifest. The recordings show actual film selection, adjustment and split comparison. Only Android status bars were removed, with proportional resizing and compression. Application controls, photo results and response delays are real. No AI-generated interface, invented processing result or replacement camera scene is used. The camera view is the Android Emulator's virtual test environment, displayed through the real app camera pipeline; the camera scene is identified as emulator footage.

Sequence: introduction → film selection → exposure / white balance controls → original / effect comparison → complete landscape workspace → dated project grid → GPU camera preview → final photo development settings → Android preview / GitHub.

The algorithm explanation in the video distinguishes GPU LUT live preview and video rendering from the complete Spektrafilm pipeline used for finished photographs. Camera preview is an approximation; spatial effects such as grain and halation are judged in the finished photograph.

## Music and typography

The warm ambient score and transition chimes are generated by `synth_music()` in the reproduction script using analytic sine tones, deterministic chords and envelopes. They contain no recorded samples, downloaded music, external sound effects or voice. The music is identical in the two editions.

Titles are rasterized using locally installed Microsoft YaHei, Segoe UI and Georgia fonts. Font binaries are not distributed. The separately generated project cover illustrations are not inputs to either video.

## Reproduction

On a system with Python 3.10+, Pillow, NumPy, OpenCV and `imageio-ffmpeg` installed:

```powershell
python tools/create_intro_video.py --orientation both
```

The script reads only `docs/media/intro-source/`, creates both final MP4 files, both JPEG posters and the shared SRT, and places intermediate files and QA sheets in the ignored `build/intro-media/` directory. To render only the layout proofs, add `--preview-only`. To render one edition, use `--orientation landscape` or `--orientation portrait`. Chinese fonts must be installed locally; update `font()` when reproducing on a different operating system.

## Validation

Both final files decoded completely with FFmpeg (exit 0), with 1,104 video frames at 24 fps and no detected black intervals (`blackdetect`, minimum 0.15 s). OpenCV independently confirmed frame count, dimensions and duration. Encoded video frames were extracted and visually inspected in both orientations. MP4 top-level atom inspection confirmed `moov` at byte 32 and `mdat` at byte 33,450 in both editions.

FFmpeg `volumedetect` measured **−26.1 dB mean** and **−14.9 dB peak** for each final AAC track, with no clipping. Validation logs and contact sheets are retained locally in `build/intro-media/`, including `intro-verification.json`, `spektramat-intro-verification.log` and `spektramat-intro-portrait-verification.log`.
